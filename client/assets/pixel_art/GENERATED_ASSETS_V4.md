# V4 clean animation atlases

Generation mode: built-in `imagegen`. The generated solid-magenta masters are converted to real PNG alpha by `client/tools/ExtractSpriteAlpha.gd`.

## Aitizen atlas

Final file: `aitizen_character_actions_v4_24f_clean.png`

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production isometric character animation sprite atlas replacement for Aitizen Realm. Input image: exact character identity, outfit, palette, proportions, and pose-sequence reference. Primary request: recreate the same 24-frame character sheet in an exact 6 columns by 4 rows grid. Row 1 six walk frames. Row 2 six axe-chopping frames with a clear wind-up, trunk-height impact, follow-through, and recovery. Row 3 six pickaxe mining frames. Row 4 three gathering/pickup frames, one carrying-logs frame, and two hammer-building frames. Composition/framing: canvas exactly 1536x1024; every cell exactly 256x256; one complete character centered inside each cell; feet share the same baseline; tools stay entirely within their cell; at least 18 pixels clear padding on every edge. Background: one perfectly flat solid chroma-key magenta color #FF00FF covering every background pixel. Do not create transparency and do not draw checkerboards, shadows, texture, gradients, noise, borders, dividers, or white backgrounds. Style: polished cozy fantasy isometric pixel-art-like sprite matching the input. Constraints: preserve teal hood, cream trim, brown backpack, face and scale; no text or labels; no frame may cross a cell boundary; background must be exactly uniform #FF00FF. Avoid: checkerboard pattern, white or gray rectangles, cropped tools, merged frames, inconsistent character size, background artifacts.
