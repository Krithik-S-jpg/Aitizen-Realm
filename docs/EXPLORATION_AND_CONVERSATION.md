# Exploration and grounded conversations

Exploration now targets known walkable tiles bordering unknown terrain, rather
than the farthest already-known location. Healthy, rested, fed citizens give it
priority over routine gathering. An exploration goal no longer silently becomes
a social visit. Normal navigation still enforces terrain knowledge and movement;
this does not reveal the map or guarantee complete exploration of disconnected
islands. Recent reported danger zones are excluded from exploration targets.

Conversations rotate between personal plans, wellbeing, diet, exploration and
resource/building discoveries. Topics have per-recipient cooldowns; the bounded
topic history persists in metadata. Dialogue is grounded template text, not new
LLM-to-LLM free-form discussion. Urgent warnings and actual community work remain
higher priority.

Attack memories retain the animal identity/species, location, witness, event ID
and observation tick. Warnings last 240 ticks and nearby awake citizens can relay
them without extending expiry. Original provenance survives relay, so hearsay is
not described as a personal attack. Important communication memories keep the
event link for inspection. Citizens warn others when they meet; a dedicated
long-distance messenger/search-for-friends behavior is not implemented.
