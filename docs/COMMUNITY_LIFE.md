# Community life

Citizens can ask a nearby citizen for spare food, agree to deliver it, walk on
known routes, and hand over real inventory. The recipient gains trust and an
important memory only after the handoff. Commitments expire or cancel when
survival needs, danger, unavailable recipients, or unreachable routes intervene.
They persist in existing world checkpoints; no database migration is needed.

Nearby conversations can relay danger warnings with their original expiry,
resource discoveries, and construction shortages. Familiar citizens are preferred
as conversation partners. Construction retains resource reservations, limits
funded sites to one builder, and exposes material-gathering claims on the job board.

Safe idle citizens carrying food may invite each other to a known lit campfire.
They walk there, wait for their companion, consume their own food once, and show
a short bowl/spoon animation. Fishing and cooking remain existing independent
survival actions, not guaranteed stages of every meal.

In Citizens, select a citizen to inspect relationships and community commitments.
Open conversations and choose a partner to filter the thread. Unread counts are
local to this app session. Click a message's speaker heading to focus that citizen.
Dialogue is factual, rule-based English; these changes do not add LLM conversation
generation. Old messages without participant IDs retain name-based compatibility.

Validation covers gifts, interruptions, warnings, shared meals, world serialization,
and a 480-tick five-citizen settlement run, plus Godot chat UI and notification tests.
Large-population performance, sustained in-game FPS, and hosted Supabase writes
are not established by these tests.
